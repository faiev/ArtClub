05 / Focus rules

ALWAYS ACTIVE

Text inputs, editable content and dialogs keep their native keyboard behavior. Browser and operating-system shortcuts are never claimed by the editor. Press Escape to close the topmost dialog/menu or cancel the current transient interaction; it must not also change tools as a side effect.
